Yes and no. The converter runs in your browser, so there is no server cost to ration and no account to create. Convert as many single invoices as you like.
What happens with scanned invoices?
This tool reads the PDF's text layer. A scanned invoice is a picture, so there is no text to read — the tool will tell you that honestly instead of returning garbage. Scanned or messy piles are what the Verified Batch service below exists for.
