Questions

Will it total my hours?
The CSV carries hours as real numbers, so your spreadsheet totals them with one SUM. The tool itself transcribes; it does not silently adjust any figure.
What kinds of timesheets work?
Any timesheet PDF with a real text layer — the kind produced by payroll and agency software. Scanned or handwritten timesheets need OCR, which the free tool does not do yet, and it will tell you that plainly.
