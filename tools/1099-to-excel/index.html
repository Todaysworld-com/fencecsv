Questions

Is it safe to use on a tax document?
The conversion happens on your own device — there is no server and no upload, and ID numbers are truncated to last-4 in the output. It is still your document: review the preview before you rely on any figure.
Which 1099 variants work?
Any variant issued as a text-layer PDF (most software-issued 1099-NEC, -MISC and -INT forms qualify). Scanned paper forms are read by in-browser OCR when there is no text layer; check the preview carefully — tax figures must be verified before you rely on them.
