Convert a utility bill PDF to CSV

Utility bills are proof of address, expense records and budget data trapped in a PDF. This converter extracts a text-layer utility bill — electricity, gas, water, internet — into a clean CSV: service period, each charge line, taxes and fees as their own rows, and the total due as printed. It re-adds the charge lines and compares them with the bill's printed total before you download, flagging any disagreement instead of hiding it. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, so a bill carrying your name, address and account number is never uploaded to a server.

The QuickBooks and Xero formats rename and reorder the columns to each platform's documented import layout (invoice and statement pages). Fields a document doesn't carry — a due date, a Xero account code — are left blank, never invented, and the invoice-total summary rows stay in the Standard format only, because both platforms count every downloaded row as a line item.

What comes out

  • Service period, account holder and bill date as printed
  • One row per charge line: usage, rate and amount where shown
  • Taxes and fees as their own rows, never buried in usage
  • Total due carried exactly as printed
  • Computed charges vs printed total, with any mismatch flagged

Every conversion ends with a verification pass: the extracted line items are re-added and compared against the document's printed total. If they disagree, the output carries both figures and a written flag. Totals are never silently "fixed" — a discrepancy you can see is worth more than a clean-looking lie. Questions

Which utilities does this work for?
Any bill issued as a digital PDF with a text layer — most electricity, gas, water and internet providers qualify. The extractor reads charge lines generically rather than assuming one provider's layout.
What if my bill is a scanned paper copy?
Scans are now read by OCR that runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded — and the tool extracts the recognized charge lines. Your provider's online account almost always offers the same bill as a digital PDF, and that version remains the most reliable input here.
