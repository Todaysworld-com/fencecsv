What comes out

  • Delivery note number and date (blank and noted if illegible — never invented)
  • Sender and receiver lines as printed
  • Line items with quantity, unit price and line total where printed
  • Computed vs printed total, mismatch flagged in writing
  • Same column layout as the invoice and purchase-order tools, for three-way matching

Every conversion ends with a verification pass: the extracted line items are re-added and compared against the document's printed total. If they disagree, the output carries both figures and a written flag. Totals are never silently "fixed" — a discrepancy you can see is worth more than a clean-looking lie.
