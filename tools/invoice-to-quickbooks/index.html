Questions

Which QuickBooks versions import CSV?
QuickBooks Online imports CSV for bills, expenses and bank data; QuickBooks Desktop imports via its own CSV/IIF utilities. The flat layout here is the common denominator for both.
What if an invoice has no number?
Then the Invoice_No field is left blank and the conversion notes say so. The tool never invents a number to fill the cell — a blank you can see beats a wrong number you can't.
