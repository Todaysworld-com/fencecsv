Emailed and exported receipts (the PDF kind from online orders, software subscriptions and travel bookings) convert to CSV here: vendor, date, each purchased item, tax, and the total — the rows your expense report or bookkeeping spreadsheet actually needs. Questions

Can it read a photo of a paper receipt?
Not yet — photos and scans need OCR, which is on the roadmap. This tool handles digital receipts (PDFs with a text layer): the kind emailed by airlines, hotels, Amazon, and software vendors.
What format do I get?
A CSV with one row per item and the receipt's header fields repeated on each row, so it filters and pivots cleanly in Excel, Sheets, or your accounting import.
