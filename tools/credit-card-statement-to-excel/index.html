Questions

Why CSV and not .xlsx?
CSV is the format Excel opens without conversion prompts, and the numbers stay numbers. If you need styled .xlsx, open the CSV in Excel and Save As — thirty seconds, same data.
What if my statement is a scan or photo?
Scans and photos are now read by OCR that runs in your browser, with nothing uploaded. Your issuer can almost always re-issue the statement as a digital PDF from online banking, and that version will work here cleanest — use OCR for the copies where it can't.
